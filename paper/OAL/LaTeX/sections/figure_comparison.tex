POV-OPD keeps dense OPD supervision but validates it with two signals: outcome validation preserves outcome-aligned token candidates and assigns only beta-capped rank weight to anti-aligned candidates, while prefix validation monitors teacher excess surprisal and decays later supervision after sustained prefix drift.}
